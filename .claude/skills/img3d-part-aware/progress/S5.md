## Deviation: subject classification (task item b; a real bug, fixed)
- Florence-2 `<CAPTION>` returned "A woman in a blue and white dress with long blonde hair." on all 5 images tested,
  ALICE included. ALICE is anime too (§3.11 expects `anime_character`).
- So the §4.5.3 keyword rules never saw the art style; after S6, `auto` would have sent anime art to Sapiens2.
- Fix: `florence2.py` uses `CAPTION_TASK = "<DETAILED_CAPTION>"` (96 tokens; "an animated girl ..." on all 5 images), and `animated` is added to `ANIME_WORDS`.
- Effect: all 4 cases are now `anime_character`. The profile stays baseline, with warning `see_through_l2d unavailable
  (vendored_source_missing); using baseline`.
- Cost: +0.3–0.8 s per warm caption. `Grounder2D` is untouched. Diagnostics: `test/.runtime/s5/diag_caption*.py`.
