## S3b fixes (ownership handed to S10 by the main session)
- `parts/labels.py`:
  - Propagation (5.3) links opposite-sheet partners at cost 1e-6, so an unvoted wall pair takes one label.
  - Two voted walls with different classes: the weaker winning vote takes the partner's label (partner bit, donor). **This deviates from §4.6.5 step 6**, which says such walls "keep their own owners (real contact)". Partners are always two walls of one thin sheet: nested garments are never "behind" each other. Needs sign-off.
  - A final partner-agreement pass, run after island merge, lets unvoted faces follow linked partners (voted first, then by confidence; up to 3 passes).
- `parts/policy.py`:
  - `plan_parts(..., partners=None)`: `_instances` unions same-class faces linked by partners, so both walls of a sheet stay one instance.
  - `_hosted` never counts faces that lie on a same-class mesh part, so `region.face_count` == the `face_part` count (fixed the anime v8 export_failed).
  - A fully cut mesh keeps its largest part on the base as a region, so the base is never empty. The executor still maps ValueError to `partition_failed`.
- Regression tests:
  - `test_parts_labels.py`: unvoted wall pairs propagate through the partner link; voted conflicts take the stronger label.
  - `test_parts_policy.py`: both walls form one instance; an overflow region never double-counts; a fully cut mesh keeps a base.
  - Repro and replay scripts: `test/.runtime/s10/{replay_assign,replay_policy,diag_pub,diag_weighted}.py` (CPU, on published layers).

## Open issue (needs a decision; no threshold tuning done)
- The v8 failures come only from tiny cut fragment instances.
  - Examples: `clothing.handwear_10` (80 faces) and `accessory.headwear_5` (100 faces) at min_part_faces 50. They are extra-view label noise next to hair, face and top.
  - On such fragments the label boundary itself breaks 20–30 % of the partner pairs; no partner rule removes that.
  - Face-weighted consistency is 0.987 on both. The minimum over parts with ≥ 4×min_part_faces is 0.917 (ALICE) and 0.953 (anime). 13 of the 31/27 cut parts are below that size.
- Options for the main session:
  - (1) a size-aware or face-weighted metric;
  - (2) a policy rule for secondary fragment instances (needs new thresholds = tuning);
  - (3) accept v8 at min > 0.9 only on 2M-face jobs (min_part_faces 200).
